# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion, with Three.js for the hero scene. Chosen because the brief recommends this stack. The user left the choice to the builder.

## Users

Business decision-makers (operations, logistics, e-commerce, healthcare, enterprise and city leaders) evaluating an autonomous delivery partner, plus general visitors curious about the company. Visitors arrive at the home page and must understand the company in seconds and be moved to contact or explore the network.

## Product Purpose

AERONEX is a futuristic autonomous logistics and delivery technology company. It runs an intelligent delivery ecosystem powered by AI, autonomous drones, robotic couriers, self-driving electric vehicles and automated warehouse infrastructure, automating the whole delivery lifecycle. The landing page presents AERONEX as a technology leader and drives partnership inquiries (Partner With Us / Get in Touch).

## Positioning

One coordinated AI that orchestrates five delivery technologies (warehouse, drones, ground robots, vehicles, sorting machines) as a single network, from order to doorstep. A competitor with a single vehicle type or a software-only dashboard could not truthfully claim the full cross-modal lifecycle.

## Operating Context

Corporate marketing site, desktop-first at a 1440px reference width (max content 1320px, 12-column grid), responsive to 1280, 768 and 390. All live operational features (network status, fleet counts, map, activity feed, tracking) are demonstration data from mock sources until real fleet APIs exist.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Scope of this build: the home (landing) page only, sections 01-12 plus navigation and footer. Other pages (Solutions, Technology, Network, About, Insights, Contact, Tracking) are deferred; nav links to them are placeholders.
- Navigation: logo with custom geometric symbol; Solutions, Technology, Network, Company, Insights; Track Delivery and Get in Touch (primary). Sticky, transparent over hero, warm white after scroll, dropdowns for Solutions and Technology, full-screen mobile menu.
- All statistics are illustrative targets and must be labelled as targets or sample metrics (24/7, 99.9% target reliability, 5 technologies, 100% AI-coordinated). Map panel values (Active Fleet 248, Deliveries 1,284, Hubs 36) are demonstration values and must be labelled.
- Must not invent real operational capabilities, certifications, customers, testimonials, or environmental impact claims. Healthcare copy must stay conditional on safety and regulatory requirements.
- Accessibility target WCAG 2.2 AA; honour prefers-reduced-motion; status never by colour alone.

## Brand Commitments

Name AERONEX. Palette pinned by the user: warm white #F8F9F7, deep charcoal #141719, cool gray #737B80, electric lime #C7F36B (sparingly: CTAs, active states, data, key highlights), deep graphite #22292D, almost black #101416 for dark sections. Sans-serif typeface from Geist, Manrope, Inter or Space Grotesk. Subtle rounded corners, avoid card grids and generic AI/purple-blue aesthetics. Inspiration (not to copy): Apple, Linear, Stripe, Tesla, Nothing, premium architecture and industrial design sites. Hero copy: "Delivery, Reimagined for the Autonomous Era."

## Evidence on Hand

None. No logo files, photography, customer proof, press, certifications or real operational data were provided. All imagery, the logo symbol and demonstration data must be authored and labelled as illustrative; the user must replace them with real material.

## Product Principles

1. Show the one coordinated intelligence, not five separate gadgets.
2. Every number is honest: targets and demo data are labelled as such.
3. Premium restraint: whitespace, precision and one accent used sparingly.
4. Human oversight and responsible automation are part of the story, not a footnote.
5. Motion explains the system and respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA target: semantic heading hierarchy, keyboard-accessible navigation and map interactions, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, meaningful alt text, status conveyed beyond colour, reduced-motion support, accessible touch targets on mobile.
