## Scope and mode

Persuade. AERONEX home page (sections 01-12, nav, footer). Visitor: business decision-maker or curious public; action: Partner With Us / Get in Touch; secondary: Explore Our Network. Proof is demonstration only: all metrics and live data are labelled targets or sample data. Other pages deferred.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a numbered drawing set for one coordinated machine network: every section is a sheet with a title block, grid references and a sheet index, and the hero is a real-time 3D model sheet with leader-line callouts. It refuses the logistics-SaaS arrangement of headline-over-stock-photo plus a row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm-white paper (#F8F9F7), charcoal ink (#141719), cool-gray annotation (#737B80), 1px hairlines, registration ticks and A-L column letters on the 12-column grid. Electric lime (#C7F36B) used only as the revision marker: CTAs, active layer, live data. Night sheets in #101416 invert the same drawing grammar. Geist for display and text, Geist Mono for title blocks, labels and figure numbers. Radii 2-4px. Recognisable without content: tick-ruled margins and a sheet-number block bottom right.

STORY: In seconds the visitor sees one AI coordinating warehouse, drone, robot and vehicle, then follows one package from order to doorstep, trusts it because targets are honest and human oversight is stated, and clicks Partner With Us.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed Three.js scene on warm paper: autonomous EV centre-right, drone upper left, ground robot foreground, architectural hub behind, three layers at different parallax speeds. Left-aligned display headline "Delivery, Reimagined for the Autonomous Era." at roughly 7vw bottom-left, sub copy beside, lime primary CTA and outline secondary directly beneath. Transparent nav on top; title block bottom right carries the Network status panel (operational, labelled demo). Leader lines with Fig. numbers tie callouts to the three machines.

FORM: Architectural drawing set, position 3 of my grounded list of seven (aeronautical chart, transit diagram, drawing set, product catalog, waybill, exploded axonometric, airport board). seed:11fbe041efbc0bca. Raises: a the sheet-index rail's lime tick tells you where "now" is (drum-machine chase light); a single scrub control (drawcord) drives the process sheet; visible baseline grid discipline (Bauhaus); registration furniture used sparingly (design annual).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction and motion

Process sheet: a 3D-lit package travels along a horizontal plan as the visitor scrolls, handing off between warehouse, drone, robot and vehicle glyphs. Motion grammar: linear plan-translations, 0.6s ease-out reveals, hairline draw-on, counters tick once. No bouncing, rotation loops or constant float. Reduced motion shows static states.
